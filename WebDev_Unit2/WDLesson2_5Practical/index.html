Ocean (photo gallery 1 theme)

An ocean is a continuous, vast body of salt water that covers approximately 71% of the Earth's surface and holds about 97% of the planet's water.

Sky (photo gallery 2 theme)

The sky is the expansive atmospheric dome visible from Earth, shifting from a bright, sunlit blue during the day to an inky expanse of stars, planets, and the moon at night. Composed of gases like nitrogen and oxygen, it scatters sunlight to create vibrant sunrises, glowing sunsets, and ever-changing formations of clouds, rain, and auroras.
